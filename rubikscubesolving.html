Step 2:

Step 2 is solving the white corners!


Now that you have your white cross,

it's time to finish the entire white side!


For this step, we are going to place the 4

white corner pieces.

(a corner piece is a piece with 3 colors)

for example:
-white + green + red
white + red + blue
white + blue + orange
white + orange + green

Let's start with 1 corner.

Find a corner piece with white in it.

For example, let's use white, green and red corner

Look at the other 2 colors: green + red

That tells you exactly where this corner needs to go.

It needs to go between the green and red center.

Put the corner underneath its correct position.

Turn the cube so the corner u wanna solve is in

the bottom-right corner.

The place where the corner nees to go should be directly

above it.

Now repeat this move:
R'D'R D
(R = right turn)
R' = right turn against the clock
D = turn down
...

Keep going until the white corner is perfectly placed.

Step 3: Solving the middle layer

Now that you have solved the white side

it is time to solve the middle layer!

The middle layer is what you need to focus on.


Your goal is to put the 4 edge pieces witouth yellow in their positions.

Thes edge pieces have 2 colors, like red & blue,

blue & orange
orange & green
green & red


Firt turn ur cube down so the yellow side is on top.

Keep your cube like this for the entire step!


Find an edge piece that doesn't have yellow.

Look at the layer of ur cube.

For example, imagine u find red + blue;

This piece belonfs between the center and the blue center.

Match the color.

of the edge

Turn only the top layer until the red part

is directly above the red center.

It should look like an ubsite down T.


Look at the other color of ur edge.

In our example it's blue.

Ask urself:
Does the blue center belong on the left or the right?


That's how u decide what algoritm to use.

If the piece needs to go RIGHT:
use: U R U' R' U' F' U F

If the piece needs to go LEFT:
use: U' L' U L U F U' F' (basicly the mirrored version of the pther algorithm)

It moves the edge into the side of the left side of the middle layer.

What do u need to do next?

After u put one edge in:
Find another edge without yellow, match it color,
decide left or right, and then use the correct algorithm!

Keep doing this until all 4 middlelayer edges are solved.

WHat it should look like ehen ur finished with this step:

Make the yellow cross.

Step 5:

Put the yellow edges in the correct positions.

Now that you have your yellow cross, it's time to put the yellow edge pieces in the correct positions.


Keep your cube with yellow on top and white on the bottom.

Look at the 4 yellow edge pieces. We want the side color of each edge to match its center piece.


1. Find a yellow edge that is already correct.

For example, if you have a yellow + blue edge, the blue side should be next to the blue center.

If one edge is already correct, put that edge at the BACK of the cube.


2. Use this algorithm:

R U R' U R U2 R'

R = right
U = up
' = turn counterclockwise
2 = turn that side twice


3. Check the yellow edges again.

Look around the cube and check if all 4 yellow edges now match their center colors.

If they don't, put a correct edge at the BACK again and do the algorithm one more time.


How do you know Step 5 is finished?

The yellow cross should still be on top, and all 4 side colors of the yellow edges should match their center pieces.

Don't worry about the yellow corners yet. We will solve those in Step 6!
