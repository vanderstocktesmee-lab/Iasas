Iasas penspinning tutorial

Pen spinning has a lot of different tricks, we will explain as much as we can abt pen spinning
and all the tricks in this tutorial.

Trick 1: Charge

The Charge is a basic pen spinning trick where the pen
spins continously between 2 fingers.

How to do it:
1. Hold the pen between ur middle finger and ring finger.
2. Keep ur fingers slightly appart.
3. Use ur middle finger to push the pen and start the rotation.
4. Let the pen spin around the space between ur 2 fingers
5. Keep ur fingers relaxed and let the pen move naturally
6. Try to keep the movement smooth and continuous.

Tip: Don't try to spin rly fast at first. Focus on keeping the rotation
going without dropping it. Once u get more confortable, u can
make the Charge faster. Explaining a pen spinnig trick in text can be rly hard, so it's prob easyer with this tut.

Trick 2: Finger Twirl

The finger Twirl is a simple trick where u can
make the pen rotate around one finger

How to do it:
1. Hold the pen loosely between ur thumb and index finger.
2. Put ur middle finger underneath the pen.
3. Push the pen gently with ur middle finger.
4. Let the pen spin around ur index finger.
5. Catch the pen when it comes back around.
6. Keep ur fingers relaxed, don't grip the pen too tightly

tip: Start with slow, controlled rotations.
Once u can do one rotation consistently, try doing several in a row.

Trick 3: Sonic

The sonic is a pen spinning trick where the pen moves
from one finger position to another while making a full rotation.

How do do it:

1. Hold the pen between ur middle finger and ring finger.
2. Keep ur fingers relaxed
3. Push the pen slightly with ur middle finger.
4. Let the pen rotate around ur middle finger.
5. While it spins, move ur index finger into position.
6. Catch the pen between ur index finger and middle finger.
7. Repeat the movemebt until it feels smooth.

The important part

Don't try to throw the pen. The sonic should be a smooth movement,
with the pen rotating around ur finger while changing positions.
